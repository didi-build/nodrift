# Breach response runbook

Use this runbook when you suspect or confirm unauthorized access to, loss of, or disclosure of personal information.

## 1. Contain and document

- Stop ongoing unauthorized access or disclosure where you can do so safely.
- Record what you know: what happened, when it was discovered, systems involved, and who is leading the response.

## 2. Assess real risk of significant harm

Under PIPEDA, assess whether the breach creates a **real risk of significant harm** to an individual. Consider the **sensitivity** of the personal information involved and the **probability** that the information will be misused.

## 3. Report to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

When the breach creates a real risk of significant harm, report it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada **as soon as feasible after the organization determines that the breach occurred**.

- OPC guidance: [What you need to know about mandatory reporting of breaches of security safeguards](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/business-privacy/privacy-breaches-at-your-business/gd_pb_201810/)
- Breach reporting form: [Report a privacy breach at your organization](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/report-a-concern/report-a-privacy-breach-at-your-organization/)

## 4. Notify affected individuals

When there is a real risk of significant harm, notify affected individuals **as soon as feasible** after you determine the breach occurred, unless law prohibits direct notification.

## 5. Notify other organizations

Notify other organizations that may be able to reduce harm (for example financial institutions, service providers, or partners) when appropriate.

## 6. Keep breach records

Keep a record of **every** breach of security safeguards involving personal information under your control, whether or not it poses a real risk of significant harm. Retain each record for **24 months** from the date the breach was determined to have occurred.

- Regulatory text (Canada Gazette, SOR/2018-64 s. 6(1)): [https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p2/2018/2018-04-18/html/sor-dors64-eng.html](https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p2/2018/2018-04-18/html/sor-dors64-eng.html)

Use `docs/privacy/breach-record-template.md` for each incident record.

_This document is a starting skeleton, not legal advice. Review it with qualified counsel before relying on it._
